# Changelog

## 0.3.0 · 2026-10-10

- Extend the historical waterfront with working quays, cargo, harbor pedestrians, and distant schooners.
- Add a three-parcel cargo job between the freight office and timber wharf, with route guidance and progress display.
- Add schooner sailing, automatic departure, steering, and automatic return and docking. Return becomes available after departure.
- Add harbor roof thickness and weathered quay surfaces.
- Preserve historical reference metadata and checksums, with local archive and verification scripts.

## 0.2.0 · 2026-10-10

- Introduce the standalone historical San Francisco experience with walking, streetcar controls, conversations, map directions, and an archival comparison.
