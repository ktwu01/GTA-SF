## Remaining criticism

The warehouses still look clean and repetitive. Dark windows and a plain horizon weaken the working-port impression. Commerce is sparse. People remain stylized. The ship is moored; cargo jobs, sailing and interiors are absent.

The building ledger records unknown historical dimensions, materials and tenants as unknown. The harbor layout is an adaptation. Precise cadastral reconstruction from legible Sanborn sheets remains outstanding. Issue8 should not be described as a completed photorealistic or surveyed1906 city.
