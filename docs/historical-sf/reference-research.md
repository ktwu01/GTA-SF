# Market Street before April 18, 1906: independent image references

Public archival material only. Source pages were browsed; all nine images below were downloaded and visually inspected. The complete pack adds the exact 1905 Ferry terminal photograph documented in `ferry-detail-research.md`. It contains 26 distinct views: 16 film frames and ten independent photographs/postcards. Its 28 image files include two supporting versions of existing captures. Public-domain status is reported with its source evidence; photochrom/postcard colors are printed interpretations, not measured facade colors. Sources earlier than 1906 establish a building's earlier appearance, not every alteration by April 1906.

## Critical period constraints

- **Film date: April 14, 1906.** The Library of Congress item identifies that date. David Kiehn's LOC National Film Registry essay gives the same date, a start at 8th Street after 3pm, and a journey to the Ferry Building with a turntable rotation. April 17 is the negative's shipment date, not filming date. [LOC item](https://www.loc.gov/item/00694408/), [Kiehn essay](https://www.loc.gov/static/programs/national-film-preservation-board/documents/Trip%20Down%20Market%20Street.kiehn.pdf).
- LOC describes Market Street as **120 feet wide**, the filmed ride as 1.55 miles, and the viewpoint as the front of a moving cable car. Use 36.576m for the nominal total street width, not automatically the entire paved carriageway. Automobile circulation was partly staged, so vehicle count is not a reliable traffic-density census. [LOC item](https://www.loc.gov/item/00694408/).
- **Chronicle clock tower absent in April 1906:** SF's official landmark report describes its destruction by bottle-rocket fire in 1905 and unfinished reconstruction when the earthquake occurred. Earlier reference 5 shows the tower and must not be copied literally. [Official landmark report](https://sfplanninggis.org/docs/landmarks_and_districts/LM243.pdf).
- Original Palace references below show the **1875–1906 hotel**, with projecting bay windows and large cornices. Do not use the 1909 replacement or its later Garden Court as exterior evidence.
- Avoid the 1937 Art Moderne Call/Central Tower facade and any post-earthquake Ferry tram balloon loop. The April 1906 film's turntable is direct evidence for the endpoint.

## Uncertainties

- `palace-c1900`: LOC's original record says **no date recorded on caption card**. Its machine date field 1900 and Commons 1900 should not be represented as a verified capture year. It is a photograph of an intact original hotel, reproduced on a Bain negative; use 'undated image of original Palace Hotel, before 1906'.
- `palace-1904`: source says 1904 and explicitly original/pre-1906. Exact January 1 is metadata year precision, not an evidenced shoot date.
- Facade colors in the three colored postcard/photochrom references were added through printing. Geometry is stronger evidence than tint. The original film has no reliable color information.
- Nominal 120 ft street width includes the street reservation; curb width, sidewalk depth, track gauge and camera position still require visual estimation. Do not claim surveyed geometry.
- Film copies can run at different speeds. Use the manifest's timestamps for this exact LOC MP4; do not transfer timestamps from Commons.
- Traffic was partly arranged for the camera. The film supports vehicle types and crossing behavior, not a precise population or normal congestion rate.

## Film frames

The original LOC viewing copy is bundled at `data/historical-sf/references/market-street-1906.mp4`. Its exact download URL is https://tile.loc.gov/storage-services/service/mbrs/ntscrm/00015143/00015143.mp4 . Sixteen frames are bundled with IDs `film-018` through`film-490`. Each records the viewing-copy offset, date April 14, 1906, Miles Brothers, LOC source item00694408 and the original source link. Public-domain status is independently supported by LOC's [2026 collection article](https://blogs.loc.gov/now-see-hear/2026/04/a-tale-of-two-films-the-san-francisco-earthquake-1906-before-and-after/).

Distinct useful frame targets: initial8th Street streetscape; close dray; opposing cable-car front; passing cable-car side; pedestrians crossing rails; bicyclist; period automobile; Call dome approach; original Palace frontage; commercial awnings/signs; Ferry clock approach; Ferry apron/terminal turntable. Visually verify each selected frame before assigning a particular building or prop name.

## Exclusions encountered

- NYPL Tom Phillips 'View down Market Street to Call Building', dated 1906, belongs to earthquake/fire scenes and shows aftermath. Exclude from intact city evidence.
- Generic NYPL 'Market Street from Ferry Depot, Chronicle and Call buildings in distance' series is in earthquake stereocards; do not accept its vague title as pre-earthquake proof.
- Ferry 1939 postcard includes Bay Bridge; NARA67th Regiment homecoming 1917–18 is much later; exclude.
- HABS 'Documentation compiled after 1933' can contain copied older images, but do not date an image 1900 without an explicit historic caption.
